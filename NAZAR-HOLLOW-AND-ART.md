# NAZAR: hollow type that reads, and pages that read as one composed piece
25 October 2026, second pass for Nadir. Twelve live sites looked at or measured this run, none repeated from GLOWUP-TYPE-NAZAR.md. Method: headless Chrome at 1440 wide, a script through Chrome's debugging protocol that reads every text element's font size, stroke width, fill, position and what sits under it, plus contact-sheet screenshots shrunk to 800 px.

## FIRST LINE, THE HONEST FINDING
Thirty-four live sites were measured for hollow type (CSS text-stroke with a transparent fill, or stroked SVG text): streetwear, motorsport, esports, festivals, foundries, the last thirty Awwwards Sites of the Day. Zero use it on their current pages. The sites with the biggest, most readable display type all set it SOLID and win their size through contrast, not through outline. So the hollow rules below are DERIVED from what the solid giants do and from the failure in Asif's screenshot, not copied from a live hollow site. I say that rather than invent a reference. Hollow stays in the site because he loves it; it becomes a texture word, never the word that carries the sentence.

## TEN RULES FOR NADIR (one line each, tied to a reference)
1. STROKE RULE, MEASURABLE: hollow stroke = 5% of the rendered font size, floor 3 px, ceiling 8% (160 px type gets an 8 px stroke; 240 px gets 12 px). Derived: at 16:1 contrast (Ballet) a 1 px hairline on 160 px type is 0.6% and vanishes; 5% matches the thin strokes of the solid weight beside it.
2. HOLLOW IS NOT ALLOWED below 96 px rendered on desktop or 72 px on the phone, and never for a word that wraps: Asif's screenshot shows the letters breaking at exactly that size; Czinger goes solid at 76 px.
3. HOLLOW IS NOT ALLOWED transparent over a photograph: either the band under the letters is held dark as Dekmantel does, or the hollow carries a 35% black tint fill with its stroke, never a clear window onto the picture.
4. One hollow word per line, and it is the connective word; the word that carries the meaning is solid (Lucid's 700 name in a 400 sentence; Signal-A last pass). Solid is loud, hollow is quiet, never the reverse.
5. The small label is never boxed and never translucent: 11 px, same left edge as the headline, 22 to 24 px above it (Dekmantel). Kill the dark box behind "other studios".
6. One idea per screen: a screen is a picture OR a sentence OR a scatter, never two (Mowalola's first screen is one photograph and a slab).
7. Scale is 16:1 between the giant and its label (Ballet 178 to 11) with ONE middle size on the page between 40 and 50 px (Czinger 48); nothing else in between.
8. Every line of type on a page shares one left edge (100 Thieves at 10, Dekmantel at 32, Koenigsegg at 80), except one scatter moment per site (Locomotive's staggered names).
9. Sections move by alternation: a black type-only screen, then a picture screen, then type again (Forge); the headline never sits on the same picture twice.
10. The orange goes on one solid word or one 10 px tag per screen (Pangram's NEW), never on a hollow stroke: a thin coloured line over a busy ground is the worst case for both readability and the accent.
