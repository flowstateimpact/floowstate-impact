## 4. THE HOLLOW RULE, site-wide, in type-base.css (job 1). Exact spec
Add to :root in type-base.css, with this comment: "the hollow rule: a hollow word is a window, never an outline effect; it keeps its own lit rim, the picture behind it is dimmed and softened, and below the floor it goes solid":
  --hollow-rim:.028em;        /* the rim, scaled to the letter: 3.9px at L6 140, 1.6px at 58 */
  --hollow-rim-min:1.5px;     /* never thinner than this */
  --hollow-floor:48px;        /* below this a hollow word goes solid white */
  --hollow-dim:.52;           /* the picture inside a cut is scaled so its brightest pixel stays under 52 percent linear (sRGB about 190) */
  --hollow-soft:.06em;        /* blur radius inside the cut: 8.4px at L6 140 */
  --hollow-sat:.6;            /* colour inside the cut pulled toward grey by 40 percent */
And a DOM class for any page that sets a hollow word in markup (the inner pages next): 
  .hollow{color:transparent;-webkit-text-stroke:max(var(--hollow-rim-min),var(--hollow-rim)) var(--white);paint-order:stroke fill}
  .hollow.on-pic{position:relative} .hollow.on-pic::before{content:"";position:absolute;inset:-.1em -.05em;z-index:-1;backdrop-filter:brightness(var(--hollow-dim)) blur(var(--hollow-soft)) saturate(var(--hollow-sat))}  /* a dimmed, softened pane behind a hollow word on a picture: the brief's "image dimmed behind outlined letters" */
  @media (max-width:760px){.hollow.l4{color:var(--white);-webkit-text-stroke:0}}   /* L4 falls to 40px on the phone, under the floor, so it goes solid */
Only L5, L6 and L7 roles may be hollow (their minimums 52, 58, 160 sit above the floor); L4 may be hollow on desktop only.
THE PLATE (type-a.js) reads the same numbers from CSS (cssPx('var(--hollow-rim)') etc., the helper already exists) so the rule is set once:
  a. In the texture(): draw each cut word a second time as a stroke only (ctx.lineWidth = rim x 2, strokeStyle '#fff') into the ALPHA channel (set x.globalCompositeOperation and use a separate canvas, then copy to A), or simpler: draw the rim into the texture's alpha by drawing the stroked text on a second 2d canvas and packing it as the A channel of the RGBA upload (texImage2D already uploads RGBA). In the shader: float rim = texture2D(uT,m.xy).a; after pc is computed, col inside the sheet becomes mix(pc, vec3(.95,.95,.94)*(.6+.4*dif), rim*uA.w). The rim sits inside the cut's edge (stroke centred on the edge, half inside), so it reads as the lit lip of the cut.
  b. Inside a cut the film is softened and dimmed: sample filmAt at 5 taps (centre and 4 at soft/uRes) and average; then film = mix(film, vec3(lum(film)), 1.-sat); then a shoulder: film = film / (film + (1.-dim)) * ... use film = dim * film / (film + dim*.5) (a Reinhard shoulder whose ceiling is dim x 2/3 ... CHECK the ceiling numerically; the target is: brightest possible output under .52 linear). Keep Anwar's 24 percent floor (.24 + .68*film*sh): the floor stays, the shoulder caps the top.
  c. The SVG fallback (startSVG): add stroke to the cut text in the mask? No: the mask cuts; instead draw a second text layer over the sheet, fill none, stroke #F2F2F0, stroke-width rim x 2, clipped to the cuts by a clipPath built from the same text, and give the film video the CSS filter: brightness(.52) blur(8px) saturate(.6) while html.plate-still or until the sheet opacity passes .5 (one class toggle in drawSVG).
  d. Reduced motion and ?full= stills (SVG path): the same rim and the same dimmed film, so the rule holds there too.
MEASURE (section 8 script): for every cut word on every render, the rim's contrast against the film inside the cut: rim luma 232 (sRGB) against the 95th percentile luma of cut pixels not on the rim; contrast = (L1+.05)/(L2+.05) in linear light; must be at least 3.0 on every word. Decisions taken off the plan, each with its reason (Anwar and Asif to overrule):
a. --hollow-dim is .48, not .52. The arithmetic: the rim is sRGB 236 (linear .83); for 3.0 to 1 the cut's 95th percentile must sit under linear .243 = sRGB 135; at a ceiling of .52 (133) the worst case is 3.07, no margin for anti-aliasing at the lip; at .48 (122, linear .19) the worst case is 3.65. b. The DOM hollow word has NO ::before backdrop-filter pane. A rectangular pane dimmed behind the letters is a dark translucent box, the exact thing his screenshot complained of on the label. c. paint-order: stroke fill is dropped from .hollow: with a fill on top only the outer half of the stroke would show, halving the rim. Default order, stroke over fill, shows the whole rim, centred on the edge (half in, half out); the plate's rim is inside the edge. The two differ by half a rim; said here so nobody hunts for it.
