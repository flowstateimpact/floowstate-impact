CONCLUDED

THE FIX, one spec for both plates (d1_plate.py centres() and build_groove(), lines 62 to 160; hero-reveal-board.html lines 52 to 65)
1. A comb pass is an arm's reach: 500 to 900px at 1440, 220 to 320px at 390, with a set-down mark (wider groove, piled burr) and a lift-out tail.
2. The next pass along overlaps by 40 to 120px and jogs 4 to 14px up or down, so a line visibly breaks and restarts. Boards never do this.
3. Each pass tilts up to 0.6 degrees and bows 6 to 10px over its length.
4. The gap between passes is 1.6 to 2.2 times the tooth spacing, so grooves group in sets, not an even ladder.
Test: a stranger at half size, "what is this surface?" If the answer is still boards, panelling or shiplap, the code stand-in has hit its ceiling and the real combed panel in raking light goes to Asif as a credit card (my 25 Oct flip).
