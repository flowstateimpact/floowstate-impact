## Verdict on v3
CHANGE. The spine is right (lit shop open, dancer as the peak, a dark music rest before the seam). Two things break it: the master's garden dissolve puts two moving pictures in one frame, and the garden walk is a wide busy frame with a small figure that reads as noise through letters. The dark singer at 2196 is also the weakest face in the loop.

## The feeling
Being let in, then dared. A stranger smiles at you, a woman dreams with her eyes shut, you see the work, the place, a dancer throws her arms up, a man points straight at you, the room drops to night and a piano, and the light comes back on the same smile. Muted, it is a day turning to night and back to morning.
