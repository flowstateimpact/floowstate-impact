# Free audit terms: one call, one written read-out, one per company

## You get one free call and one written read-out, and each company takes part once

THE SHORT VERSION

The free audit is exactly one video call of up to 60 minutes and one written read-out. We send the read-out by email within 10 working days after the call. There are 10 places in total. They go strictly in the order complete applications arrive, by date and time. Each company takes part only once. You owe us nothing and are under no obligation to buy anything.

A. WHO CAN TAKE PART

1. One free audit per company, once
Each company can take part in the free audit only one time.

2. Related companies count as one
Companies with the same owner, the same group or the same management count as one company for this purpose. They share a single place if they apply together or separately.

3. No re-applying under another name
A company that has had its audit, or lost its place under these terms, cannot apply again. This includes using another name, a new trading name, or asking another person to apply on their behalf.

4. Who can apply on your behalf
The person applying must be the owner, a director, or someone the owner has allowed to apply. That person must also have permission to share business details with us during the process.

5. People working for a competing studio or consultancy cannot take part
People who run or work for a competing studio or consultancy cannot take part. This includes applying for them directly or on behalf of their clients. If you are found to work for a competing studio or consultancy, the audit will end.

## The 10 places go in the order applications arrive, and a place gives you one call and one read-out

B. THE WAITING LIST

6. Ten places go in the order complete applications arrive There are exactly 10 places available for the free audit. Places go strictly in the order complete applications arrive, by the date and time we receive them. No other factor decides which place goes first.

7. What makes an application complete An application is complete when the form is filled in and the contact details work. We confirm a place on the list by email within 2 working days of receiving your application.

8. A place belongs to your company only The place belongs to the specific company that applied for it. It cannot be sold, swapped, or handed to another business. You also cannot exchange it for money, a discount, or any paid alternative.

9. What happens when places are full When all 10 places are filled, new applicants stay on the waiting list in order. If a place frees up later, it goes to the next person on the list. Being on the list does not promise you a place.

10. When the offer ends The offer runs for a limited time with no fixed end date. It ends when the 10 places are used, or earlier if we close it. If we close it, we say so on this page and tell everyone still on the list by email. A place on the list does not carry over to any later offer unless we say so in writing.

C. WHAT YOU GET, AND WHAT YOU DO NOT

11. The exact contents of the audit The audit consists of one video call of up to 60 minutes and one written read-out. We send this read-out by email within 10 working days after the call.

12. How we count working days Working days for us are Sunday to Thursday, not counting public holidays in Bangladesh. If something outside our control delays the read-out, we say so and give a new date.

13. We do not do the work suggested We do not carry out any of the work the read-out suggests. This means no design, writing, building, or strategy work is included in the free offer. The audit includes no extra calls, no rewriting of the read-out, and any questions are limited to the one email in the next sentence. You may send one email of questions about the read-out within 14 days of receiving it, and we answer it once.

14. The read-out is final The read-out is a finished document, not a draft to be tweaked. We do not rewrite it to match a view you prefer or change its structure later. If we got a fact wrong through our own mistake, tell us and we correct that specific fact only.

15. Paid work is separate If you want paid work later, it is a new and separate agreement with its own written scope and terms. Nothing in the free read-out is a price, a quote, or a promise of what paid work would include.

## Your part is true details, a kept call, and respect for what each side owns

D. WHAT WE NEED FROM YOU

16. The read-out rests on what you share
We may send a short set of questions before the call. If the information you give is incomplete or wrong, the read-out will be too. We are not responsible for that gap.

17. Giving false details ends the audit
This applies if you use a false company name, state a false role, or hide that you work for a competing studio or consultancy.

E. MOVED, MISSED OR LATE CALLS

18. To move the call, tell us at least 24 hours before You can move it once. If you ask to move the call a second time, the place goes to the next on the list.

19. If you do not join and did not tell us
We wait 15 minutes. Then the call is closed and the place passes to the next on the list.

20. If you join late
The call still ends at its booked time.

21. If we need to move the call
We tell you as early as we can and offer a new time. You keep your place.

F. WHO OWNS WHAT

22. You keep your read-out
You may use it for your own business. You may share it inside your company and with your own advisers, such as your accountant or lawyer.

23. Our tools stay ours
Our questions, the way we structure the read-out, our templates, scoring and working tools stay the property of FlowState Impact. Receiving a read-out gives no right to them.

24. You may not sell or publish the read-out
It may not be sold, posted publicly, used to train software, presented as your own work, or used to give the same service to others.

25. Using our name needs permission
Using our name, the read-out or quotes from it in marketing or the press needs our written permission first.

26. No recording without agreement Neither of us records or shares the call unless we both agree first. This includes AI note-taking tools and meeting bots. We take our own written notes.

## You owe us nothing, your details stay private, and any ending or change is told to you

G. ADVICE, RESPONSIBILITY AND NO OBLIGATION

27. The audit is not legal, financial, tax or accounting advice The call and read-out give our view on your business. They do not replace professional advice. For those specific areas, speak to a qualified professional.

28. The read-out reflects our honest view The read-out is our honest view on the day it is written. We promise no result from following it. We are not responsible for decisions you make or losses that follow, as far as the law allows. Nothing here removes rights that the law says cannot be removed.

29. You owe us nothing and we do not chase you You owe us nothing and are under no obligation to buy anything. We are under no obligation to offer paid work. We may tell you once what paid work would look like. We will not chase you, and nothing renews or charges automatically.

H. PRIVACY

30. Your details stay within the studio What you share is used only to prepare your audit. We do not share it outside the studio, except where the law requires it. A trusted contractor who works for us may also see your details, and they are held to the same duty of privacy as the studio.

31. We ask before using your story If we want to use a lesson from your audit with your name and details removed, for example in an article, we ask you first in writing. Using your name or company in a case study needs a separate written yes.

32. Audit material is deleted after twelve months We keep audit material for 12 months, then delete it, unless you become a paid client. Data protection rights you have where you live still apply.

33. We do not add you to mailing lists We do not add you to a mailing list unless you say yes.

I. WHEN WE MAY DECLINE, PAUSE OR END AN AUDIT

34. We tell you if a rule is broken We may decline, pause or end an audit when a rule here is broken: false details (17), working for or applying for a competing studio or consultancy (5), recording or sharing without agreement (26), resale or misuse of the read-out (24), or rude, threatening or abusive behaviour towards anyone in the studio. When we do, we tell you which rule was broken.

35. If your audit would use private information from another client, we tell you and cannot run it. If that conflict ends while the offer is still open, you keep your place on the waiting list.

J. GENERAL

36. Changes to the offer are posted here We may change or end the offer. Changes are posted on this page with the date. A change never takes away a place already confirmed, or the call and read-out already promised to someone holding one.

37. These terms are the whole agreement These terms and your booking confirmation are the whole agreement for the audit. If one clause turns out not to be valid, the rest still stand.

38. Disputes follow Bangladeshi law These terms follow the law of Bangladesh, and the courts of Dhaka decide any dispute. Before going to court, both sides first try to settle a problem in writing for 30 days.
