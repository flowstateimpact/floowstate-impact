B. THE WAITING LIST

6 Places go in the order complete applications arrive There are 10 places. They go to complete applications by the date and time we receive them. First come, first served. Nothing else decides the order.

7 The confirmation email arrives within 2 working days An application is complete only when the form is filled in and your contact details work. Within 2 working days we send you the confirmation email. It says one of two things: that you hold a place, or your position on the waiting list. When you hold a place, the confirmation email, or a follow-up in the same email thread, also gives the date, time and form of your call.

8 Your place belongs to your company A place belongs to your company. It cannot be sold, swapped or handed to another business. It cannot be exchanged for money, a discount or any paid alternative.

9 A freed place goes to the first person on the waiting list Once all 10 places are held, new applicants join the waiting list, in order. If a place frees up, it goes to the first person on the waiting list. This happens if someone withdraws, misses the call under clause 18 (You can move your call once, with 24 hours' notice) or clause 19 (A missed call closes after 15 minutes), or loses the place under clause 34 (Five rules, and what happens when one is broken). The first person on the waiting list is told by email. Being on the waiting list does not promise a place.

10 The offer ends after the tenth call, or when we end it The offer is for a limited time, with no fixed end date. It ends at the first of two moments: when the tenth call has taken place, or when we end the offer under clause 36 (We may change or end the offer, but a place already held is kept). A place counts as used once its call has taken place. A place freed before its call goes back to the waiting list under clause 9 (A freed place goes to the first person on the waiting list). When the offer ends, the waiting list closes. Everyone still on it is told by email within 5 working days. Their position ends. It does not carry over to any later offer unless we say so in writing. People who already hold a place keep it under clause 36 (We may change or end the offer, but a place already held is kept).

C. WHAT YOU GET, AND WHAT YOU DO NOT

11 One call and one written read-out The audit is one call of up to 60 minutes, by video. It includes one written read-out, sent by email within 10 working days after the call.

12 Working days run Sunday to Thursday Working days are Sunday to Thursday, not counting public holidays in Bangladesh. If something outside our control delays the read-out, we tell you and give a new date.

13 We do not carry out the work the read-out suggests We do not carry out any of the work the read-out suggests. There is no design, writing, building, or strategy work. There are no extra calls, unlimited follow-up questions, or rewriting of the read-out. You may send one email of questions about the read-out within 14 days of receiving it. We answer it once.

14 The read-out is finished, not a draft The read-out is a finished document, not a draft. We do not rewrite it to match a view you prefer. If we got a fact wrong because of a mistake on our side, tell us and we correct that fact.

15 Paid work is a separate agreement If you later want paid work, it is a new and separate agreement. Nothing in the read-out is a price, a quote or a promise of what paid work would include. Paid work has its own written scope and terms.
