34 Five rules, and what happens when one is broken
When one of five rules is broken, we may take an application off the waiting list, or pause or end an audit. We use this power for these five reasons only, never for any other. The five rules are:
1. Providing false details, which clause 17 (False details break these terms) describes.
2. Working for or applying for a competing studio, as set out in clause 5 (People from competing studios cannot take part).
3. Recording or sharing the call without agreement, under clause 26 (No recording or sharing of the call unless both sides agree).
4. Reselling, publishing or misusing the read-out, covered by clause 24 (You may not resell, publish or pass off the read-out).
5. Rude, threatening or abusive behaviour towards anyone in the studio.

We tell you in writing which rule was broken. A pause is a hold used while we check something. During a pause you keep your place, and we tell you when we will decide. If an audit ends before its call, the place goes to the first person on the waiting list, as clause 9 (A freed place goes to the first person on the waiting list) explains.

35 Your audit waits while a conflict of interest lasts
A conflict of interest means that running your audit would mean using what another client told us in private. If that happens, we tell you that we cannot run your audit while the conflict lasts. You keep your position on the waiting list. If the conflict ends while the offer is still open, your audit goes ahead in its turn.
