# Terms of the free audit

## You get one free call and one written read-out, and each company gets one

Here is the whole deal in one breath. You get one video call of up to 60 minutes. We then send you one written read-out by email within 10 working days. That is everything included. Places go strictly in the order complete applications arrive. Each company gets only one place. If you are on the waiting list, that position promises nothing. You owe us nothing for this offer.

You get one free call and one written letter about your business, so you do not have to buy anything afterwards because it costs you nothing. There are 10 places, and they go in the order complete applications arrive. One company gets one place, no matter how many people work there. If you are on the waiting list, you are not promised a place yet.

WORDS USED ON THIS PAGE

We, us, and the studio mean FlowState Impact, registered in Dhaka, Bangladesh. A place means one of the 10 free audits. Holding a place means you will get the call and the read-out. The read-out is the written letter about your business that we send after the call. The waiting list is the line of people who have applied but do not yet hold a place. Being on it is not a place.

A. WHO CAN TAKE PART

1 One free audit for each company, once Each company can take part in this offer one time. Once your company has had its audit or lost its place, you cannot apply again.

2 Companies with the same owner, group or management count as one If several companies share the same owner, group, or management team, they count as one company for this offer. They do not get separate places.

3 No second application under another name A company that has already applied cannot try again under a new name or through another person.

4 The owner, a director or someone the owner allows must apply The person filling in the form must be the owner, a director, or someone the owner has allowed to apply and share business details.

5 People from competing studios cannot take part If you run or work for a business that sells branding, design, or business consulting to other companies, you cannot take part. This includes applying on behalf of your own clients. If this comes to light later, clause 34 (Five rules, and what happens when one is broken) applies. We may end the audit and tell you why.

## Places go in order, and the audit stops at one call and one read-out

B. THE WAITING LIST

6 Places go in the order complete applications arrive There are 10 places. They go to complete applications by the date and time we receive them. First come, first served. Nothing else decides the order.

7 The confirmation email arrives within 2 working days An application is complete only when the form is filled in and your contact details work. Within 2 working days we send you the confirmation email. It says one of two things: that you hold a place, or your position on the waiting list. When you hold a place, the confirmation email, or a follow-up in the same email thread, also gives the date, time and form of your call.

8 Your place belongs to your company A place belongs to your company. It cannot be sold, swapped or handed to another business. It cannot be exchanged for money, a discount or any paid alternative.

9 A freed place goes to the first person on the waiting list Once all 10 places are held, new applicants join the waiting list, in order. If a place frees up, it goes to the first person on the waiting list. This happens if someone withdraws, misses the call under clause 18 (You can move your call once, with 24 hours' notice) or clause 19 (A missed call closes after 15 minutes), or loses the place under clause 34 (Five rules, and what happens when one is broken). The first person on the waiting list is told by email. Being on the waiting list does not promise a place.

10 The offer ends after the tenth call, or when we end it The offer is for a limited time, with no fixed end date. It ends at the first of two moments: when the tenth call has taken place, or when we end the offer under clause 36 (We may change or end the offer, but a place already held is kept). A place counts as used once its call has taken place. A place freed before its call goes back to the waiting list under clause 9 (A freed place goes to the first person on the waiting list). When the offer ends, the waiting list closes. Everyone still on it is told by email within 5 working days. Their position ends. It does not carry over to any later offer unless we say so in writing. People who already hold a place keep it under clause 36 (We may change or end the offer, but a place already held is kept).

C. WHAT YOU GET, AND WHAT YOU DO NOT

11 One call and one written read-out The audit is one call of up to 60 minutes, by video. It includes one written read-out, sent by email within 10 working days after the call.

12 Working days run Sunday to Thursday Working days are Sunday to Thursday, not counting public holidays in Bangladesh. If something outside our control delays the read-out, we tell you and give a new date.

13 We do not carry out the work the read-out suggests We do not carry out any of the work the read-out suggests. There is no design, writing, building, or strategy work. There are no extra calls, unlimited follow-up questions, or rewriting of the read-out. You may send one email of questions about the read-out within 14 days of receiving it. We answer it once.

14 The read-out is finished, not a draft The read-out is a finished document, not a draft. We do not rewrite it to match a view you prefer. If we got a fact wrong because of a mistake on our side, tell us and we correct that fact.

15 Paid work is a separate agreement If you later want paid work, it is a new and separate agreement. Nothing in the read-out is a price, a quote or a promise of what paid work would include. Paid work has its own written scope and terms.

## Share honest details, keep your call time, and keep the read-out for your own business

16 The read-out rests on what you share
We need honest, complete information to make the read-out useful. We may send a short set of questions before the call. If the information is incomplete or wrong, the read-out will be too, and we are not responsible for that.

17 False details break these terms
This includes a false company name, a false role, or hiding that you work for a competing studio. If we find false details, clause 34 (Five rules, and what happens when one is broken) applies: we may take the application off the waiting list, or pause or end the audit, and we tell you why. An honest mistake that you correct when we ask is not a false detail.

18 You can move your call once, with 24 hours' notice
If you need to move the call, tell us at least 24 hours before. The first move keeps your place. A second move means the place goes to the next person on the waiting list.

19 A missed call closes after 15 minutes
If you do not join the call and did not tell us, we wait 15 minutes, then the call is closed. The place passes to the next person on the waiting list.

20 A late start still ends at the booked time
If you join late, the call still ends at its booked time.

21 If we move the call, you keep your place
We tell you as early as we can and offer a new time. You keep your place.

22 You keep your read-out
You keep the read-out for your own business use. It may be shared inside your company and with your own advisers, for example your accountant or lawyer.

23 Our methods stay ours
The questions we ask, how we structure the read-out, our templates, scoring and working tools remain the property of FlowState Impact. Receiving a read-out does not give you any right to them.

24 You may not resell, publish or pass off the read-out
It cannot be sold, posted publicly, used to train software, presented as your own work, or used to deliver the same service to others.

25 Our name or the read-out in marketing or the press needs our written permission
Using our name, the read-out or quotes from it in marketing or the press needs our permission in writing first.

26 No recording or sharing of the call unless both sides agree
No recording or sharing happens unless both sides agree first. This includes AI note-taking tools and meeting bots that join the call. We take our own written notes.

This audit is an honest view of your business, not professional advice. For those specific matters, speak to a qualified professional.

The read-out is our honest view on the day it is written. We do not promise any result from following it. We are not responsible for decisions you make or losses that follow from it, as far as the law allows. Nothing here removes rights that the law says cannot be removed.

29 You owe us nothing, and we will not chase you
You owe us nothing for the audit and are under no obligation to buy anything. We are under no obligation to offer you paid work. We may tell you once what paid work would look like. We will not chase you, and nothing renews or charges automatically.

What you share is seen only by the people who prepare your audit, and we use it only to prepare your audit. The people who see it are our own team. We pass it to nobody else, with one exception: when the law requires us to, and then we tell you, unless the law forbids telling. The services that store our email and files are named in our privacy notice (clause 32).

31 We ask in writing before using a lesson from your audit
If we want to use a lesson from your audit with your name and details removed, for example in an article, we ask you first in writing. Using your name or company in any case study needs a separate written yes.

32 We keep audit material for 12 months, then delete it
Your personal details are handled under our privacy notice. We keep audit material for 12 months, then delete it, unless you become a paid client.

33 No mailing list unless you say yes
We do not add you to a mailing list unless you say yes.

## Breaking one of five rules can end an audit, and these terms follow Bangladesh law

34 Five rules, and what happens when one is broken
When one of five rules is broken, we may take an application off the waiting list, or pause or end an audit. We use this power for these five reasons only, never for any other. The five rules are:
1. Providing false details, which clause 17 (False details break these terms) describes.
2. Working for or applying for a competing studio, as set out in clause 5 (People from competing studios cannot take part).
3. Recording or sharing the call without agreement, under clause 26 (No recording or sharing of the call unless both sides agree).
4. Reselling, publishing or misusing the read-out, covered by clause 24 (You may not resell, publish or pass off the read-out).
5. Rude, threatening or abusive behaviour towards anyone in the studio.

We tell you in writing which rule was broken. A pause is a hold used while we check something. During a pause you keep your place, and we tell you when we will decide. If an audit ends before its call, the place goes to the first person on the waiting list, as clause 9 (A freed place goes to the first person on the waiting list) explains.

35 Your audit waits while a conflict of interest lasts
A conflict of interest means that running your audit would mean using what another client told us in private. If that happens, we tell you that we cannot run your audit while the conflict lasts. You keep your position on the waiting list. If the conflict ends while the offer is still open, your audit goes ahead in its turn.

36 We may change or end the offer, but a place already held is kept
We may change or end the offer. Changes are posted on this page with the date. If we end the offer, the waiting list closes as clause 10 (The offer ends after the tenth call, or when we end it) describes. A change or an ending never takes away a place already held. It also does not remove the call and read-out already promised to someone holding one.

37 These terms and your confirmation email are the whole agreement
These terms and your confirmation email, which clause 7 (The confirmation email arrives within 2 working days) defines, form the whole agreement for the audit. Nothing said in a chat, a call or another message adds to them. If one clause turns out not to be valid, the rest still stand.

38 Bangladesh law applies, and we talk before going to court
These terms follow the law of Bangladesh, and the courts of Dhaka decide any dispute. If a problem arises, both sides first try to settle it by talking, in writing, for 30 days. Only if that fails does it go to the courts of Dhaka. This does not take away any right that the law of your own country gives you and does not allow to be taken away.
