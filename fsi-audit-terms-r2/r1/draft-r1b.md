# Terms of the free audit

THE SHORT VERSION

You receive one video call of up to 60 minutes. We send one written read-out by email within 10 working days. Places go in the order complete applications arrive. Each company gets one place. This offer costs you nothing.

WORDS USED ON THIS PAGE

"We", "us" and "the studio" mean FlowState Impact, registered in Dhaka, Bangladesh.
"A place" means one of the 10 free audits. Holding a place means you will get the call and the read-out.
"The waiting list" is the line of people who have applied but do not yet hold a place. Being on it is not a place.
"The read-out" is the written letter about your business that we send after the call.
"The confirmation email" is the email of clause 7. It is the only document besides these terms that forms part of the agreement (clause 37).

A. WHO CAN TAKE PART

1 One chance per company
Each company can take part in this offer one time.

2 Linked firms count as one
If several companies share the same owner, group, or management team, they count as one company for this offer. They do not get separate places.

3 No second application under another name
A company that has had its audit or lost its place under these terms cannot apply again under a different name, a new trading name, or through another person.

4 Who can fill the form
The person filling in the form must be the owner, a director, or someone the owner has allowed to apply and share business details.

5 Not for rival studios
If you run or work for a business that sells branding, design, or business consulting to other companies, you cannot take part. This includes applying on behalf of your own clients. If we learn this afterwards, see clause 34 (Five rules and results).

B. THE WAITING LIST

6 Places go in the order complete applications arrive
There are 10 places. They go to complete applications by the date and time we receive them, first come, first served, and nothing else decides the order.

7 Your place status
An application is complete only when the form is filled in and your contact details work. Within 2 working days, we send the confirmation email. It says one of two things: that you hold a place, or your position on the waiting list. When you hold a place, the confirmation email, or a follow-up in the same email thread, also gives the date, time and form of your call.

8 Not for resale
The place cannot leave your company. It cannot be sold, swapped or handed to another business. It cannot be exchanged for money, a discount or any paid alternative.

9 Next person gets place
Once all 10 places are held, new applicants join the waiting list, in order. If a place frees up, it goes to the first person on the waiting list. This happens if someone withdraws, misses the call under clause 18 (Move call once, 24 hours) or clause 19 (Missed call closes in 15 minutes), or loses the place under clause 34 (Five rules and results). The first person on the waiting list is told by email.

10 Offer ends after tenth call
The offer is for a limited time, with no fixed end date. It ends at the first of two moments: when the tenth call has taken place, or when we end the offer under clause 36 (Offer changes keep your place). A place counts as used once its call has taken place. The waiting list closes when the offer ends. Everyone still on it is told by email within 5 working days, and their position ends without carrying over to any later offer unless we say so in writing.

C. WHAT YOU GET, AND WHAT YOU DO NOT

11 One call and one written read-out
The audit is one call of up to 60 minutes, by video. It includes one written read-out, sent by email within 10 working days after the call.

12 Working days run Sunday to Thursday
Working days are Sunday to Thursday, not counting public holidays in Bangladesh. If something outside our control delays the read-out, we tell you and give a new date.

13 We do not carry out the work the read-out suggests
The audit does not cover design, writing, building, strategy, extra calls, or unlimited questions. You may send one email of questions about the read-out within 14 days of receiving it. We answer it once.

14 Final document
The read-out is finished and not a draft. We do not rewrite it to match a view you prefer. If we got a fact wrong because of a mistake on our side, tell us and we correct that fact.

15 New terms
Paid work happens under a new and separate agreement. Nothing in the read-out is a price, a quote or a promise of what paid work would include. Paid work has its own written scope and terms.

D. WHAT WE NEED FROM YOU

16 Please give us honest and complete details
We need honest, complete information to make the read-out useful. We may send a short set of questions before the call. If the information is incomplete or wrong, the read-out will be too, and we are not responsible for that.

17 Give us true details
This includes a false company name, a false role, or hiding that you work for a competing studio. If details turn out to be false, see clause 34 (Five rules and results). An honest mistake that you correct when we ask is not a false detail.

E. MISSED OR LATE CALLS

18 Move call once, 24 hours
If you need to move the call, tell us at least 24 hours before. The first move keeps your place. A second move means the place goes to the next person on the waiting list.

19 Missed call closes in 15 minutes
If you do not join the call and did not tell us, we wait 15 minutes, then the call is closed. The place passes to the next person on the waiting list.

20 Fixed end time
The call ends at the booked time if you join late.

21 If we move the call, you keep your place
We tell you as early as we can and offer a new time.

F. WHO OWNS WHAT

22 You keep your read-out
You keep the read-out for your own business use. It may be shared inside your company and with your own advisers, for example your accountant or lawyer.

23 Our methods stay ours
The questions we ask, how we structure the read-out, our templates, scoring and working tools remain the property of FlowState Impact. Receiving a read-out does not give you any right to them.

24 You may not resell, publish or pass off the read-out
It cannot be sold, posted publicly, used to train software, presented as your own work, or used to deliver the same service to others.

25 Public use
Using our name, the read-out or quotes from it in marketing or the press needs our written permission first.

26 Consent required
Both sides must agree before recording or sharing the call. This includes AI note-taking tools and meeting bots that join the call. We take our own written notes.

G. ADVICE, RESPONSIBILITY AND OBLIGATION

27 This is not legal, financial, tax or accounting advice
The audit is not legal, financial, tax, or accounting advice. Consult a qualified professional for these areas.

28 Your decisions are yours
The read-out is our honest view on the day it is written. We do not promise any result from following it. We are not responsible for decisions you make or losses that follow from it, as far as the law allows. Nothing here removes rights that the law says cannot be removed.

29 You owe us nothing, and we will not chase you
You are under no obligation to buy anything. We are under no obligation to offer you paid work. We may tell you once what paid work would look like. We will not chase you, and nothing renews or charges automatically.

H. PRIVACY AND CONFIDENTIALITY

30 Who sees your details
Only our audit team views your information, and we use it solely to prepare your audit. The people who see it are our own team. We pass it to nobody else, with one exception: when the law requires us to, and then we tell you, unless the law forbids telling. The services that store our email and files are named in our privacy notice (clause 32).

31 We ask in writing before using a lesson from your audit
If we want to use a lesson from your audit with your name and details removed, for example in an article, we ask you first in writing. Using your name or company in any case study needs a separate written yes.

32 Storage limit
Your personal details are handled under our privacy notice. We keep material for 12 months, then delete it if you do not become a paid client.

33 Optional list
We do not put you on a mailing list unless you say yes.

I. WHEN A RULE IS BROKEN

34 Five rules and results
When one of five rules is broken, we may take an application off the waiting list, or pause or end an audit. We limit ourselves to these five reasons and no others. The five rules are:
(1) Providing false details, clause 17.
(2) Working for, or applying for, a competing studio, clause 5.
(3) Recording or sharing the call without agreement, clause 26.
(4) Reselling, publishing or misusing the read-out, clause 24.
(5) Rude, threatening or abusive behaviour towards anyone in the studio.

We tell you in writing which rule was broken. A pause is a short wait while we check whether a rule was really broken. During it you keep your place, and we give you a date by which you will hear the result.

35 Your audit waits while a conflict of interest lasts
A conflict of interest means that running your audit would mean using what another client told us in private. If that happens, we tell you that we cannot run your audit while the conflict lasts. You keep your position on the waiting list. If the conflict ends while the offer is still open, your audit goes ahead in its turn.

J. GENERAL

36 Offer changes keep your place
We may change or end the offer. Changes are posted on this page with the date. If we end the offer, the waiting list closes as clause 10 (Offer ends after tenth call) describes. A change or an ending never takes away a place already held.

37 Full terms
These terms and your confirmation email, which clause 7 (Your place status) defines, form the whole agreement for the audit. Nothing said in a chat, a call or another message adds to them. If one clause turns out not to be valid, the rest still stand.

38 Bangladesh law applies, and we talk before going to court
These terms follow the law of Bangladesh, and the courts of Dhaka decide any dispute. If a problem arises, both sides first try to settle it by talking, in writing, for 30 days. Only if that fails does it go to the courts of Dhaka. This does not take away any right that the law of your own country gives you and does not allow to be taken away.
