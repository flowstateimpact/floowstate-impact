# Fact check: the Bangladesh data law the terms and privacy notice cite (P172, 25 Oct 2026)

Claim checked (ILYAS-R1.md line 105): "Bangladesh's Personal Data Protection Ordinance 2025 was published in the official gazette on 6 November 2025."

## Result: TRUE FOR 2025, OUT OF DATE NOW
- The ordinance was real: Ordinance No. 61 of 2025, dated 6 Nov 2025 on bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/act-1574.html and in the Extraordinary Gazette of November 2025 (dpp.gov.bd). TBS reported it on 9 Nov 2025 (tbsnews.net/node/1281356).
- It has since been repealed and re-enacted by the Thirteenth Jatiya Sangsad as the Personal Data Protection Act 2026.
  - Justice Corner BD, 19 Oct 2026 (justicecornerbd.com/blogs/e-commerce-laws-in-bangladesh-and-legal): "Personal Data Protection Act 2026 (Act No. 63 of 2026)", regulator the National Data Management Authority; says the 2025 ordinance references "should accordingly be read as superseded".
  - Also named as the Personal Data Protection Act 2026 by New Age (16 Jul 2026), Bangladesh Law Digest (Aug 2026) and a ResearchGate paper (28 Sep 2026).
- Act number 63 rests on one source (Justice Corner BD). Not yet read: the Act's own text on bdlaws (the page served binary to the reader), so its commencement date and the exact notice duty are UNCHECKED.

## What the writing route must do
- Any terms or privacy sentence that names the law says "the Personal Data Protection Act 2026", never the 2025 ordinance.
- Do not state the act number or a specific section unless the Act's text is read first.
- Ilyas's conclusion stands: a privacy notice is needed before terms.html ships. Whether the Act legally requires it today is unconfirmed until its commencement is read; the notice ships either way, because the terms point to it.
