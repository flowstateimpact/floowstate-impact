# Free audit terms page: brief for the local writer (flowlong), revision 2

Built 25 Oct 2026 by P164 from TERMS-SPEC-BILAL.md revision 2 (Bilal, operations), which fixed the four contradictions Layth's lint found. Facts only; every body sentence is written by the local model.

PURPOSE: The star beside "Free for a limited time" on every audit button links to this page. It says plainly what the free audit is, what it is not, and what each side can expect, so nobody is surprised later, and so nobody can turn a free audit into free consulting.

READER: A small business owner in Dhaka or London who has never met us, reading on a phone, maybe in a second language. A six-year-old should be able to say back the deal: you get one call and one written letter about your business, it is free, you wait your turn, one per company. Calm and respectful, like a host explaining the house rules, never a guard reading out penalties.

RIVAL: The usual terms and conditions page: dense legal words ("hereinafter", "the Client shall", "notwithstanding"), capital-letter walls, written to win a court case rather than to be understood. This page must be the opposite: every clause readable in one pass, with the legal idea said in everyday words.

REGISTER: Measured professional, child-clear in meaning. Complete sentences. Short paragraphs. No slogans, no selling, no jokes. No em dashes. Never mention Akij. Never say or hint that the studio chooses, judges or declines who gets a place: no shortlisting, curating, selecting, declining, "successful applicants". Places go in order. The page never praises the studio and never sells it.

FORMAT: Open with THE SHORT VERSION: four or five plain sentences (one free call, one written read-out, waiting-list order, one per company, no obligation). Then WORDS USED ON THIS PAGE (five short definitions, unnumbered). Then the full terms in ten groups, A to J, in the order below. Each clause gets its own short plain heading that states its point (for example "You keep your read-out", never "Intellectual Property"), followed by one to three sentences. Keep the clause numbers and order. When a clause points to another clause, name it by its number and its heading. Target 1,600 to 1,900 words in total.

FACTS (use these and nothing else; never add a number, a promise or a party that is not here):

Company: FlowState Impact, a business registered in Dhaka, Bangladesh. Never describe the legal form of the business. No registration number and no street address appear on the page.

WORDS USED ON THIS PAGE
- "We", "us" and "the studio" mean FlowState Impact, registered in Dhaka, Bangladesh.
- "A place" means one of the 10 free audits. Holding a place means you will get the call and the read-out.
- "The waiting list" is the line of people who have applied but do not yet hold a place. Being on it is not a place.
- "The read-out" is the written letter about your business that we send after the call.
- "The confirmation email" is the email of clause 7. It is the only document besides these terms that forms part of the agreement (clause 37).

A. WHO CAN TAKE PART
1. One free audit per company, once.
2. Companies under the same owner, the same group or the same management count as one company for this offer.
3. A company that has had its audit, or lost its place under these terms, cannot apply again under a different name, a new trading name or through another person.
4. The person applying must be the owner, a director, or someone the owner has allowed to apply and to share business details.
5. People who run or work for a competing studio or consultancy cannot take part, including applying on their behalf or for their clients. A competing studio means a business that sells branding, design or business consulting to other companies. If this comes to light later, clause 34 applies: we may end the audit, and we tell you why.

B. THE WAITING LIST
6. There are 10 places. Places go to complete applications by the date and time we receive them, first come, first served. Nothing else decides the order.
7. An application is complete only when the form is filled in and your contact details work. Within 2 working days we send you the confirmation email. It says one of two things: that you hold a place, or your position on the waiting list. When you hold a place, the confirmation email, or a follow-up in the same email thread, also gives the date, time and form of your call.
8. A place belongs to your company. It cannot be sold, swapped or handed to another business, and it cannot be exchanged for money, a discount or any paid alternative.
9. Once all 10 places are held, new applicants join the waiting list, in order. If a place frees up (someone withdraws, misses the call under clause 18 or 19, or loses the place under clause 34), it goes to the first person on the waiting list, who is told by email. Being on the waiting list does not promise a place.
10. The offer is for a limited time, with no fixed end date. It ends at the first of two moments: when the tenth call has taken place, or when we end the offer under clause 36. A place counts as used once its call has taken place; a place freed before its call goes back to the waiting list under clause 9. When the offer ends, the waiting list closes. Everyone still on it is told by email within 5 working days, their position ends, and it does not carry over to any later offer unless we say so in writing. People who already hold a place keep it (clause 36).

C. WHAT YOU GET, AND WHAT YOU DO NOT
11. The audit is one call of up to 60 minutes, by video, and one written read-out, sent by email within 10 working days after the call.
12. Working days are Sunday to Thursday, not counting public holidays in Bangladesh. If something outside our control delays the read-out, we tell you and give a new date.
13. We do not carry out any of the work the read-out suggests. There is no design, writing, building, strategy work, extra calls, unlimited follow-up questions, or rewriting of the read-out. You may send one email of questions about the read-out within 14 days of receiving it, and we answer it once.
14. The read-out is a finished document, not a draft. We do not rewrite it to match a view you prefer. If we got a fact wrong because of a mistake on our side, tell us and we correct that fact.
15. If you later want paid work, it is a new and separate agreement. Nothing in the read-out is a price, a quote or a promise of what paid work would include. Paid work has its own written scope and terms.

D. WHAT WE NEED FROM YOU
16. We need honest, complete information, and the read-out rests on what you share. We may send a short set of questions before the call. If the information is incomplete or wrong, the read-out will be too, and we are not responsible for that.
17. False details break these terms. This includes a false company name, a false role, or hiding that you work for a competing studio. If we find false details, clause 34 applies: we may take the application off the waiting list, or pause or end the audit, and we tell you why. An honest mistake that you correct when we ask is not a false detail.

E. MISSED OR LATE CALLS
18. If you need to move the call, tell us at least 24 hours before. You can move it once. After that, the place goes to the next person on the waiting list.
19. If you do not join the call and did not tell us, we wait 15 minutes, then the call is closed. The place passes to the next person on the waiting list.
20. If you join late, the call still ends at its booked time.
21. If we need to move the call, we tell you as early as we can and offer a new time. You keep your place.

F. WHO OWNS WHAT
22. You keep the read-out for your own business use. You may share it inside your company and with your own advisers, for example your accountant or lawyer.
23. Our methods stay ours. The questions we ask, how we structure the read-out, our templates, scoring and working tools remain the property of FlowState Impact. Receiving a read-out does not give you any right to them.
24. You may not resell, publish or pass off the read-out. It cannot be sold, posted publicly, used to train software, presented as your own work, or used to deliver the same service to others.
25. Using our name, the read-out or quotes from it in marketing or the press needs our permission in writing first.
26. No recording or sharing of the call unless both sides agree first. This includes AI note-taking tools and meeting bots that join the call. We take our own written notes.

G. ADVICE, RESPONSIBILITY AND OBLIGATION
27. This is not legal, financial, tax or accounting advice. For those, speak to a qualified professional.
28. Your decisions are yours. The read-out is our honest view on the day it is written. We do not promise any result from following it, and we are not responsible for decisions you make or losses that follow from it, as far as the law allows. Nothing here removes rights that the law says cannot be removed.
29. You owe us nothing for the audit and are under no obligation to buy anything. We are under no obligation to offer you paid work. We may tell you once what paid work would look like; we will not chase you, and nothing renews or charges automatically.

H. PRIVACY AND CONFIDENTIALITY
30. What you share is seen only by the people who prepare your audit, and we use it only to prepare your audit. The people who see it are our own team. We pass it to nobody else, with one exception: when the law requires us to, and then we tell you, unless the law forbids telling. The services that store our email and files are named in our privacy notice (clause 32).
31. If we want to use a lesson from your audit with your name and details removed, for example in an article, we ask you first in writing. Using your name or company in any case study needs a separate written yes.
32. Your personal details are handled under our privacy notice. We keep audit material for 12 months, then delete it, unless you become a paid client.
33. We do not add you to a mailing list unless you say yes.

I. WHEN A RULE IS BROKEN
34. When one of five rules is broken, we may take an application off the waiting list, or pause or end an audit. This is the only clause that says what happens after a rule is broken; clauses 5, 17, 24 and 26 point here. The five rules: (1) false details, clause 17; (2) working for or applying for a competing studio, clause 5; (3) recording or sharing the call without agreement, clause 26; (4) reselling, publishing or misusing the read-out, clause 24; (5) rude, threatening or abusive behaviour towards anyone in the studio. We use this power for these five reasons only, never for any other. We tell you in writing which rule was broken. A pause is used while we check something; during a pause you keep your place, and we tell you when we will decide. If an audit ends before its call, the place goes to the first person on the waiting list (clause 9).
35. A conflict of interest means that running your audit would mean using what another client told us in private. If that happens, we tell you that we cannot run your audit while the conflict lasts. You keep your position on the waiting list, and if the conflict ends while the offer is still open, your audit goes ahead in its turn.

J. GENERAL
36. We may change or end the offer. Changes are posted on this page with the date. If we end the offer, the waiting list closes as clause 10 describes. A change or an ending never takes away a place already held, or the call and read-out already promised to someone holding one.
37. These terms and your confirmation email (clause 7) are the whole agreement for the audit. That means nothing said in a chat, a call or another message adds to them. If one clause turns out not to be valid, the rest still stand.
38. These terms follow the law of Bangladesh, and the courts of Dhaka decide any dispute. If a problem arises, both sides first try to settle it by talking, in writing, for 30 days. Only if that fails does it go to the courts of Dhaka. This does not take away any right that the law of your own country gives you and does not allow to be taken away.
