হেরিটেজ ইন মোশনHeritagein Motion

A concept fashion house. The studio made its own film, then built the house from it.

Sixty-one seconds, from Old Dhaka to a sandbar on the Jamuna.

  • Concept house
  • AI fashion film
  • Identity
  • 2026

The film

A cut. Ask and we will send the whole film

Dhaka

The film came first. A woman in black walked out of Old Dhaka, crossed the Jamuna and reached a sandbar at dusk, carrying a painted bag. When it was finished, the film had a house in it, so we built the house.

We kept every frame inside Bangladesh: the lane, the river, the char. We chose black khadi for the coat and left the cloth undyed where it showed. We gave the house one mark, the eye stitched in the film’s last frame, and one rule for it. The eye is only ever seen through an opening, looking out.

“Dhaka dressing Dhaka the way Paris dresses Paris.”

Asif Farid, founder, on the stance

We wrote no story for the cloth and no explanation for the garment. The house does not translate itself. It has no shop and no customers. What exists is the film, the mark and the frames on this page.
