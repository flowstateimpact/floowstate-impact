ছায়াChhaya

A phone network that says one small thing, then stops talking.

A concept brand. The studio built it from its own film, The Lane. There is no network, and nothing here is for sale.

The brand was built from this advert. Each part of it below opens on a frame of the film.

The Lane · AI film · 42 seconds · sound

Even here.

The Lane is a chase with nothing chasing. A boy runs through a lane at night, climbs a wall and goes into a pond, holding one thing above the water: his phone. The signal holds. The last card says two words.

“the biggest claim in the fewest words, calm and plain”

Asif Farid, founder, on the stance

We built the brand from that card. A network has one large thing to say, and we chose to say it small and say it once.

Then we asked where a person in this country goes to be calm. They go into the shade. Chhaya means shade, and it means shelter. So the brand became a cool room in a hot city, where the phone works and nobody raises their voice about it.

Under one roof

The film ends on a card, so the identity started there. Bangla letters hang from a line along their top. We made that line thick enough to stand under, and kept one lamp lit beneath it.

Two grounds, one lamp

In the film the only light is the phone and the only colour is one red. The brand kept both. By day it is a limewashed wall and a bamboo blind. By night it is the dark on the inside of that blind. The lamp is the same red each time, and it is always light coming through something.

Bangla leads

One family carries both scripts: Anek Bangla. Bangla sets the size. Latin follows a step smaller, as the gloss, and never the other way round.

যেখানে সেখানে

Anywhere, everywhere: full net, full speed.

In the hand

The film has one object in it: a phone in a hand. The brand was made at that size first, the pack, the card and the screen. The wall and the bus came after.

Three places

A hall room, an exam night, a bus at Farmgate. Each place was photographed three ways: the people, the hand, the pack.

The set

One lamp, carried from the site to the tile, the post, the story and the shop front.

The map

A specimen. Chhaya has no towers, so this is a drawing of a manner and never a survey: the brand marks where it has not reached as plainly as where it has.

যেখানে ছায়া পৌঁছেছে, সেখানে ছায়া।
যেখানে পৌঁছায়নি, সেখানে এখনো রোদ।

Where Chhaya has reached, there is shade. Where it has not, there is still sun.

One speed

Nothing in Chhaya moves faster than shade does. The same hall room at two in the afternoon and at twenty to six. The only thing that travelled is the light.
