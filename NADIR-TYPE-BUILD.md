## What each variant is (for the note Anwar reads)
A THE PLATE. Letters are cuts in a blackened steel sheet; the lane film plays behind and shows only through the cuts; DARE is sprayed on in orange; the who block is printed on the plate as the bracket (bold line 24px above the cap top, dim lines 24px below the last baseline). On scroll the plate hinges away at its top edge, recedes and rises, and the film floods the screen; DARE rides its own plane a beat behind, the last to lift. WebGL ray-casts every pixel to the hinged plane; the plate's thickness throws a shadow onto the film inside each cut at the top left (light from the top left), the rim catches light, a brushed grain and one sheen move with the tilt. Fallback (no WebGL, or a double-clicked file whose film cannot be read as a texture): the same layout as an SVG sheet masked by the letters, hinged by CSS perspective. Real h1 and who block stay in the document, visually hidden. What we do: YOUR WHOLE BUSINESS, at L4 then READ. at L6 orange (2 to 1), both arriving through the cut, READ. half a second later; on desktop the four row pictures are moved into one stack at four depths, pinned at the right, each turning like a page as its row is read.
DIMENSION: a sheet with a hinge and a thickness you see in the cut shadow. DEPTH: film, plate, paint, three real layers lit from one side. CHARACTER: the studio's own stencil plate, cut once and sprayed through; the work is behind the words. STYLE: steel and film, no glow, no chrome. SIGNATURE: the plate lifting and DARE staying. UNIQUENESS: no studio hero shows its film through its own letters.
B THE LEDGER. Hush then the cut: We make the work at L3 top left in the text face (14ch, two lines), other studios at L2 right, 24px above the stencil line, WILL NOT DARE at L6 bottom right on the fold, DARE orange; the who block holds the bottom left. WILL, NOT, DARE sit at -160, 0, +120px in a 1200px perspective; each has a three-step cut edge down-right of the light and a soft shadow for when it lands on the street; the line turns up to 2 degrees toward the pointer, and on scroll the far plate holds back while DARE leaves first. The lower bracket is read as the film edge itself (the line is cut by the fold on purpose); said here because it departs from the fixed 24px. What we do: the title runs up the left edge in three columns at L4 (READ. orange) on the spine of a slab seen in perspective; a one-screen pin turns the slab 78 to 0 degrees to face the reader, the cover lightens as it comes to the light, and the title lies flat at L5 across the top. The stair of pictures hangs one screen lower to make room for the turn; the first picture does not enter on the first screen (it would break the stair that hangs from the page), it arrives as the cover faces you.
DIMENSION: three plates at three measured depths, one slab with two faces. DEPTH: parallax by depth, light on the cover as it turns. CHARACTER: a ledger read corner to corner, then a book turned on the table. STYLE: Tschichold's diagonal in the two faces. SIGNATURE: the slab turn. UNIQUENESS: the sentence travels the whole screen; nobody's hero is a book spine.
C THE SMALL LINE. The whole sentence at L2, one line, centred on the vertical axis, DARE orange; behind it DARE alone at L7, orange at 18 percent, blurred 6px, 600px back, cut by the right edge, its cap top 24px under the sentence, the who block bottom left. On scroll the word is pinned in the window, comes 600px forward, sharpens and fills with colour over the first screen, and is in focus exactly as the street is under it; it then leaves with the film. What we do: General Sans 400 at 92px on one line, no stencil until TAKE ONLY WHAT YOU NEED at the close; the full stop is the mark3d flame plate turning in its place; a 2px orange line reads under the title left to right once on load (a static hairline under reduced motion).
DIMENSION: one object in a room, far then near. DEPTH: focus and distance, not layers. CHARACTER: restraint with one extreme. STYLE: posh by withholding. SIGNATURE: the giant arriving in focus on the street. UNIQUENESS: the only big thing is out of focus.
